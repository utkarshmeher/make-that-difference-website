A LITTLE ABOUT ME

True healing begins when we learn to consciously live, not just cope.

I believe every person deserves a safe, compassionate space to pause, understand their inner world and find a way forward that feels true to them.

My work brings together psychological understanding and inner exploration. With a Post Graduate Diploma in Psychology & Psychopathology, NLP training and over a decade of experience in the corporate world, I bring empathy, structure and practical insight to the work we do together.

Over the last three years, I have supported 40+ clients through concerns including anxiety, emotional regulation, relationships, identity transitions, self-worth and personal growth.
